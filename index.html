About the publication

We tell the story
behind the business.

Navodya is a digital publication focused on founder stories, entrepreneurial journeys, startup struggles and business developments.

Beyond the success headline.

We believe a business story is bigger than a launch, a valuation or a milestone. It is also the uncertainty before a decision, the sacrifices behind a result, the rejection that changed a plan and the lessons learned while building something from scratch.

Our aim is to give those stories a thoughtful place — from young entrepreneurs and women business leaders to small businesses, bootstrapped startups and emerging industries.

We want to be useful to readers, fair to the people we cover and careful with the facts we publish.

01 / ORIGINALITY

Stories, not press releases.

We aim to add reporting, context and a clear editorial angle.

02 / VERIFICATION

Check the details.

Names, figures, dates and company claims should be verified and attributed.

03 / TRANSPARENCY

Disclose paid work.

Sponsored content must be clearly labelled and separated from independent reporting.

04 / ACCOUNTABILITY

Correct the record.

Material errors should be corrected transparently.

How we work

Editorial standards.

A proposed editorial framework based on Navodya's project brief. It should be reviewed and formally approved by the publisher before launch.

Accuracy before urgency.

Every article should be original and properly researched. Verify names, statistics, company information and dates. Attribute information to its source, distinguish claims from independently verified facts and avoid fabricated quotes or exaggerated achievements.

Storytelling with context.

Articles should focus on the real story rather than simply promoting a company. Include relevant context, explain what is known and what remains uncertain, and seek a fair response where appropriate.

Transparency and corrections.

Clearly disclose paid or sponsored content. Correct material errors visibly and promptly. Do not imply that payment guarantees favourable independent coverage.

REPORTING CHECKLIST

Before an article goes live

Confirm spelling, titles, dates and figures.

SOURCE CHECK

Keep a source trail

Record links, documents, interview dates and image credits.

EDITORIAL REVIEW

Review the framing

Check headlines for accuracy, fairness and unnecessary hype.
